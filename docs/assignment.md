## Design Systems (`/design-systems/<style>`), one per mood board
1. **Color roles:** background, surface, text, muted text, border, accent, with contrast ratios.
2. **Type roles:** display, heading, body, label, with sizes and weights, set in our own content.
3. **Spacing & shape:** spacing scale, corners, borders, shadow (and motion: e.g. "200ms, ease-out, no bounce").
4. **Components:** primary + secondary buttons, link, text input, select, checkbox, radio, toggle,
   tabs, filter chips, a card with one of our items, list rows, badge, modal or detail panel,
   plus anything the site needs.
5. **Control states:** rest, hover, focus, pressed, selected, disabled (where they apply).
6. **UI states:** empty, loading, error, success.

## The Site (`/<site-route>`)
1. Thoughtful visual hierarchy.
2. At least two faked interactions (e.g. filter + detail panel).
3. Every control in its states.
4. Accent kept for the primary action.
5. Responsive: phone 390px and desktop 1440px (also check 320).

## Design Requirements
- **Visual hierarchy:** it's clear what to look at first.
- **Type:** a few sizes and weights, used consistently, comfortable to read.
- **Color:** a small palette where each color has a purpose.
- **Accessibility:** body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Don't rely on color alone (add a word or an icon).
- **Layout:** intentional alignment, related things grouped, works on desktop and mobile.
- **Affordances:** interactions are obvious.
- **States:** controls respond to hover, focus, and press.
- **Visual style:** looks like the chosen style, not the agent's default.
- **Simplicity:** nothing extra. If a word or image doesn't help, remove it.
